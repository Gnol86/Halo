## A natural light profile from the start

Fresh installations now include a saved **Natural light** profile, ready to assign to room lights. Its linear curves follow the sun's elevation: brightness rises from **40% at −20°** to **100% at 20°**, and white temperature from **2,000 K at 0°** to **5,500 K at 20°**. Morning and evening are linked initially.

Rename, adjust or delete the profile like any other. It is saved during installation, before opening the panel or configuring a room. Reloading Halo does not duplicate it or recreate a deleted profile. Existing installations keep their profiles, including an empty list. Light assignments and room automation remain explicit choices.

## Installation and validation

Requires **Home Assistant 2026.10.0 or newer**. Update through the Halo custom **Integration** repository in HACS, restart Home Assistant and reload your browser. For manual installation, use `custom_components/halo` from this release's source archive; the compiled panel is included.

The local Python suite passes **549 tests**, including initial profile creation, immediate persistence, editing, deletion, reinstallation, compatibility with existing settings and failed writes. Ruff and formatting checks pass. The release workflow additionally requires Python checks, TypeScript and panel tests, a reproducible panel build, Hassfest and HACS validation before publication.

Halo remains in early development. The [default HACS catalog submission](https://github.com/hacs/default/pull/11771) is awaiting review. Publication does not install this update in Home Assistant, and simulated-device checks do not validate household hardware.
