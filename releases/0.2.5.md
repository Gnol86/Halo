## Recognize delayed Hue group reports

Hue reports a group's power separately from the colors and brightness computed from its bulbs. After Halo switches a group off, the last bulb can already be off while the group still reports on and changes from white temperature to XY color. Halo 0.2.4 incorrectly interpreted this as a manual change and paused the room.

Halo now remembers the available members of each commanded Hue group. Confirmed member shutoff identifies the delayed group completion, including reports beyond the nominal transition duration. For brightness and color adjustments, changed members must match their individual command expectations, and the group must match the current member average. This also recognizes late averages when individual bulbs reach the final target at different times.

Identified user and external commands retain priority, whether reported on the group or a changed member. Unexpected individual changes cannot hide inside the group's rounding tolerance. New commands replace previous expectations; a later physical turn-on after confirmed shutoff still pauses automation. Transition bounds and final-target tolerances are unchanged.

## Scope and validation

The household shutoff sequence was captured in native Activity and state history, and the Hue integration's aggregation behavior was checked in Home Assistant's source. Regression tests reproduce both the color-mode fallback after shutoff and delayed member averages. **656 Python tests** pass, including **19 new group-feedback cases**, with Ruff and formatting checks passing. The panel and its compiled bundle are unchanged. Publication also requires the panel, HACS and Hassfest workflow checks.

Group recognition needs available member entities and a group update. Selecting the individual bulbs gives Halo their individual events, including changes that may not alter a rounded group average. Unidentified physical actions matching expected feedback remain indistinguishable. Unexplained changes hours after a command are not treated as Halo feedback by this fix; their source still needs investigation.

Requires **Home Assistant 2026.10.0 or newer**. Update through HACS, or copy `custom_components/halo` from the source archive, then restart Home Assistant when convenient. Publication and installation do not prove that the new code is loaded or fully validated on household hardware.
